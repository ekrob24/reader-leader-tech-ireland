# Global comparable winning agentic hackathon entries

## Google Cloud ADK Hackathon, 2025
Source: https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/adk-hackathon-results-winners-and-highlights

Google reports 10,400+ participants from 62 countries, 477 submitted projects, and 1,500+ agents. The focus was multi-agent orchestration for process automation, data analysis, customer service, and content generation.

Named winners and honorable mentions:
- SalesShortcut: multi-agent SDR system for lead generation, research, proposal generation, and outreach; grand prize.
- Energy Agent AI: multi-agent energy customer-management workflow; North America regional winner.
- Edu.AI: multi-agent Brazilian education system evaluating essays, generating study plans, and creating interdisciplinary mock exams; Latin America regional winner.
- GreenOps: AI team continuously auditing, forecasting, and optimising cloud infrastructure for sustainability; Asia-Pacific regional winner.
- Nexora-AI: personalised education through interactive lessons, visuals, quizzes, and AI support; EMEA regional winner.
- Particle Physics Agent: converts natural language into validated Feynman diagrams using physical laws and high-fidelity data; honorable mention.
- TradeSageAI: multi-agent financial analysis and trading-hypothesis evaluation; honorable mention.
- Bleach: visual AI agent builder that describes, designs, and tests agents in plain English; honorable mention.

## NVIDIA NeMo Agent Toolkit Hackathon, 2025
Source: https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/hackathon-winners-bring-agentic-ai-to-life-with-the-nvidia-nemo-agent-toolkit/

The two-week hackathon judged real-world applicability, technical execution, and how convincingly the projects demonstrated agentic AI. First place was a Route Optimization Agent using specialised agents for natural-language constraint extraction and route computation, a solver, simulation, and deployment to robots. Second place OpenCodeReview used orchestration, memory, and swappable models to automate secure code review. Third place was a multi-step travel planner with APIs, memory, fallback data access, maps, and booking workflows. An honorable mention was a Cyber Agent with modular sub-agents for logs, network activity, and processes, coordinated into a security analyst workflow.

## AI Tinkerers Humans-in-the-Loop Hackathon, Seattle, 2024
Source: https://www.geekwire.com/2024/the-winning-ideas-from-ai-tinkerers-human-in-the-loop-ai-agent-hackathon-in-seattle/

Top prize MetabolixAI used AI to combine glucose-meter data, meal images, and preferences into personalised nutrition insights and meal planning. Runner-up AI DevRel Project monitored forums, assessed opportunities, drafted responses, and retained human quality control. Community pick LeadScore scored inbound leads with human assistance to improve assessment. This supports a pattern: the best agentic products do bounded autonomous work and make the human’s next decision faster or better.

## Cross-hackathon winning signals
1. A painful workflow is clearer than a vague social mission.
2. The agent has tools, state, constraints, and an observable handoff—not just a chat interface.
3. Winners demonstrate a complete loop from input to action or recommendation.
4. Domain-specific evidence, rules, or solvers make the system more credible than generic prompting.
5. Human-in-the-loop is strongest when the human’s role is designed, not added as a disclaimer.
6. Real-world scale, cost, speed, or risk reduction makes the pitch commercially legible.
7. A memorable demo moment is supported by a repeatable product workflow.
